04 The Studio Publishing · Games · Film

We make images that feel like places you've been before — and worlds you want to stay in.

NIGHTQUILL is an independent art studio built on a simple idea: every story deserves a world you can step into. From the first thumbnail to the final varnish, we treat each piece as a collaboration between colour, character and feeling.

Our work moves between book covers, comics, editorial illustration and character design — always with the same obsession: images that stay with you after the page is closed.
